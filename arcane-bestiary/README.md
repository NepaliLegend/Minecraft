# Arcane Bestiary

A Fabric mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1** that adds new mobs, three summonable bosses, a full crystalite tool and armor set, and four legendary boss weapons.

## Content

### Mobs
| Mob | Where | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Frost Wraith** | Snowy biomes | Ranged. Throws ice bolts that slow and freeze. Immune to freezing, weak to fire. Drops Frost Essence. |
| **Ember Imp** | The Nether | Fire-immune. Spits fireballs, bites to ignite, hurt by water. Drops Ember Essence. |
| **Crystal Crawler** | Overworld caves / darkness | Spider variant with crystal spikes that cut melee attackers. Drops Crystalite Shards. |

### Bosses
Each boss has a boss bar, a short invulnerable summoning intro, a per-hit damage cap, slow regeneration out of combat, and an **enrage phase below 50% health** (new texture, faster attacks, minion summoning).

| Boss | Summon with | Abilities | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Infernal Warden** | Infernal Sigil | Fireball volleys, telegraphed Inferno Nova, summons Ember Imps when enraged | Emberbrand, Infernal Core |
| **Glacial Sovereign** | Glacial Sigil | Ice-bolt barrages, Blizzard (area freeze), short-range teleport, summons Frost Wraiths when enraged | Frostfang, Glacial Core |
| **Crystal Behemoth** | Crystal Sigil | Crystal spike lines, telegraphed ground slam, thorns, hatches Crystal Crawlers when enraged | Behemoth Maul, Crystal Heart |

Sigils are used by right-clicking the ground. They do not work in Peaceful.

### Tools, weapons and armor
* **Crystalite** sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe and full armor set (diamond-tier). Crystalite Ore generates underground.
* **Emberbrand** - ignites targets; right-click throws a fireball.
* **Frostfang** - slows and freezes targets; right-click releases a frost nova.
* **Behemoth Maul** - every hit sends a shockwave through nearby enemies.
* **Tempest Staff** - right-click calls lightning on whatever you look at; sneak + right-click calls a storm. Crafted from an Infernal Core, a Glacial Core, a Crystalite Shard and two Blaze Rods.

### Solar Panel
A flat panel that measures real sunlight (sky light minus ambient darkness), so it is weaker at dusk and dead at night, in the Nether, under cover, and in heavy rain.
* **Redstone:** emits a signal of 0-15 equal to the sunlight level.
* **Repair:** right-click with a damaged tool, weapon or armor piece to place it inside; it is repaired in daylight (up to 3 durability per second at full sun). Right-click with an empty hand to take it back. Breaking the panel drops the stored item.
* **Crafting:** Solar Cell (2 glass panes, 2 quartz, 1 crystalite shard) x3 + 2 iron ingots + redstone + 3 smooth stone slabs.

Everything is in the **Arcane Bestiary** creative tab, including spawn eggs for every mob and boss.

## Building and running

Requires Java 21.

```bash
./gradlew build          # jar in build/libs/
./gradlew runServer      # dev server
./gradlew runClient      # dev client
./gradlew runGameTest    # automated tests (spawn, boss abilities, sigils, weapons)
```

## Assets

Textures, models, recipes, loot tables, tags, worldgen and the language file are generated by `tools/generate_assets.py` (needs Pillow) and committed under `src/main/resources`. Edit the script and rerun it to change them:

```bash
python3 tools/generate_assets.py
```
